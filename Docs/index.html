BrowseAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-22

BrowseAI is a browser extension that provides a local “web memory” layer (trails, missions, replay, and highlights). This policy explains what data BrowseAI handles and how it is used.

Summary
  • Stored locally: BrowseAI stores data on your device (in your browser’s extension storage / IndexedDB).
  • No selling / advertising: BrowseAI does not sell data and does not use your data for advertising.
  • No third‑party sharing: BrowseAI does not share your data with third parties.

Data BrowseAI collects (on-device)

BrowseAI may store:

  • Browsing activity data: page URLs, page titles, domains, timestamps, visit counts, and time spent (where available).
  • Navigation trails: relationships between pages (e.g., “from URL → to URL”) to power trails and replay.
  • Missions/session metadata: groups inferred from your activity (mission id/title, created/updated times, associated domains/pages).
  • User-created highlights: text you explicitly highlight and save, plus the page URL and timestamp.
  • Settings: your preferences (e.g., excluded/paused domains, whether to track in incognito, export redaction preferences).

BrowseAI does not intentionally collect passwords, credit card numbers, or form field contents.

How BrowseAI uses data

  • Show trails when you revisit a page.
  • Provide searchable browsing memory views (where available).
  • Generate missions and enable mission export/reopen.
  • Provide trail replay and navigation continuity.
  • Save and re-open your highlights.

Data sharing and transfers

  • BrowseAI does not transmit your browsing data to external servers.
  • BrowseAI does not share data with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until you delete it.
  • You can delete data using Clear All Data, Forget Page, and Pause Site (exclude domain).

Incognito / private browsing

BrowseAI does not track incognito browsing unless you enable “Allow in incognito” for the extension in Chrome and enable incognito tracking In BrowseAI settings (if present).

Security

BrowseAI stores data locally in your browser. No method of storage is 100% secure. Avoid saving highlights that contain highly sensitive information.

Children’s privacy

BrowseAI is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

If you have questions, do contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store listing contact method for BrowseAI.
